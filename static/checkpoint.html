Exact Settings

Scenario: standard layout, fixed door width 0.6, 30 high-mobility agents, 10 low-mobility agents, max_ticks 500, dt 0.1.
Seeds: [0, 1, 2].
Search: random search 50 samples, GA population 15, GA generations 15.

Checkpoint Objective

fitness = total_time + 0.05 * mean_congestion + 0.001 * near_collisions + fairness_weight * fairness_gap_time + 10.0 * remaining_agents

Fairness is G = abs(mean_low_time - mean_high_time). Lower fitness is better.
